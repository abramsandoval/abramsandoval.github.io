You Have Worth

"I will praise thee; for I am fearfully and wonderfully made."

Psalm 139:14

Your past does not define you. Your mistakes do not determine your worth. You were created in the image of God, and your life has meaning and purpose. Through Jesus Christ, there is hope, grace, forgiveness, and a new beginning.

Discover Your Worth

You Were Created With Purpose

YouHaveWorth.org exists to remind people that their identity and worth are not found in the world, but in God. We are created in His image, loved by Him, and invited to know Him through Jesus Christ. I am here to encourage you, share God's Word, and help you discover the hope and purpose found in Christ.

Created in His Image

Every human life has value because we are created in the image of God. Your worth is not based on success, possessions, popularity, or the approval of others.

Genesis 1:27

Loved by God

God demonstrated His love through Jesus Christ. Even though we are sinners, Christ died for us so that we may be reconciled to God.

Romans 5:8

Made for a Purpose

God has created us in Christ Jesus for good works. Your life is not meaningless. God calls us to walk in faith, love others, and glorify Him.

Ephesians 2:10

"For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus unto good works, which God hath before ordained that we should walk in them." Ephesians 2:10 — KJV
